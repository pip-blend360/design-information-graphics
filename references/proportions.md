# Percentages and Proportions

## Choose

Use divided (100% stacked) bars to compare compositions across groups. Use pie charts for a small number of mutually exclusive parts of one whole when approximate share is sufficient. Use visual tables with numbers and restrained in-cell marks when lookup and precision matter. Compare independent percentages with ordinary bars, dots, or lines instead.

## Require

Require categories, supplied shares or counts, units, denominator definition, and group IDs where applicable. Verify nonnegative values and declared whole coverage, allowing documented rounding tolerance. Do not compute shares, normalize incomplete totals, or infer denominators. Ask upstream for prepared shares if needed.

## Design and check

Order categories meaningfully; keep category colors consistent. Label shares directly, avoid 3D/exploded pies, and do not encode percentages as squared radius. Keep segment order consistent across bars. Check whole coverage, label fit, small segments, and whether unequal group sizes need supplied sample-size labels. A visual table's marks must share a declared scale.
